    An electronic quest for one Warden against the machine

    Obsidian Spire

    Take the Sun, Moon and Star shards from the barrows of the Gloaming Reach, storm the Spire, and kindle its crown before Ser Vael of Frostvale does. If the Gloom fills its track first, nobody wins.

    Drag to turn the board, scroll or pinch to zoom. Press V or the Warden's eye button to see the Reach in first person. Keys: Y yes, N no, X next, F fight, L flee, S stay, I ledger, R repeat, E end.

    Chapter I

    The Kindling Road

      How to play

      The Obsidian Spire is waking. Kindle its crown with the three shards of the old sky, the Sun, Moon and Star, before Ser Vael does and before the Gloom fills its track.

      Your turn

      Click a glowing space to ride on to the next space (two with a Swift Steed), or press Stay to remain where you are. Every road has a space of its own halfway along, so each journey between two places takes two days. In the wilds or out on a road, staying means making camp and foraging. Wherever you stop, the Spire tells you what happens and asks for your choices on its keypad. After your action your company eats: one provision for every twenty soldiers or part of twenty. Run out and men desert.

      The spaces

      Your keep
      Hearthmoor gathers 3 coin of tithes each round you are away, up to 36. Men-at-arms cost 2 here, provisions 3 for five.
      Market towns
      Sellswords at 3 coin, provisions at 4 for five, and three relics, different in each town.
      Shrines
      Pray for pilgrims to join you (once every five rounds), pay the oracle to learn which shard a barrow hides, or buy a ward that turns aside one wraith, fever or curse.
      Barrows
      Wights hold the antechamber. Beat them and the sigil on the tomb door shows which shard lies below. A Barrow King guards the tomb itself. Win there and the shard is yours. Six barrows hide two shards of each kind.
      The wilds
      Brigands, wolves, wraiths, fog, storms, fever, caches, sellswords, pilgrims, peddlers and omens. Each province also breeds its own beast: Bog Trolls in Saltmere heal their wounds as you fight, Giant Spiders in Thornwood carry venom, Cinder Imps in Emberwold steal coin, and Dune Scorpions in Sunscar have shells that blunt your blows. Frost Ogres hit hard but carry loot; they are rarer, because Frostvale's own marchers drive them out, just as yours keep Hearthmoor's roads clear.
      On the road
      The small studs halfway along each road. Mostly quiet, but anything the wilds can throw at you can happen here too. Camp to forage.
      Border crossings
      Where a road leaves one province for the next, a garrison of marchers holds a gatehouse, marked on the board by a watch-post and a diamond in both provinces' colours. Pay their toll, Fight your way through (they guard a toll chest), or take the wild way round, where the land itself takes men or provisions: tides in Saltmere, ash vents in Emberwold, drifts in Frostvale, thorns in Thornwood, dunes in Sunscar, bogs in Hearthmoor. Your own marchers let you through free on Hearthmoor's borders; Ser Vael's hold Frostvale's, and they are tougher and dearer. A ride never slips past a border: it stops at the gate. Lose the fight and flee, and you are driven back the way you came.
      The Spire
      Sealed until you carry all three shards. Then take the gate from its Sentinels, beat the Ash Warden in the hall, and slay the Crown Dragon coiled about the peak, whose fire burns several of your company every time it wins an exchange. The climb is one push: turn back or flee before the dragon falls and every guardian rises again, so muster a large company before you go in. They grow stronger as the Gloom deepens. With the dragon dead, repeat the song of kindling note for note on the three sockets. A wrong note throws three of your company from the parapet, and the crown sings a new song for the next try.

      Battles

      Each press of Fight is one exchange. The bigger your company against the foe, the likelier you win it. Win and the foe loses several; lose and the foe cuts down about one of your company for every six points of strength it still has, so a great host hits far harder than a band of brigands. Fierce, venomous and fire-breathing foes take more. Flee at any time for a provision and the risk of one more loss. If your company is wiped out you are routed: back to your keep with five survivors and half your purse, and the Gloom rises. When a battle ends, its result stays on the Spire until you press Next (or Enter).

      Relics

      • Wraith Lantern: wraiths cannot touch you.
      • Swift Steed: ride two roads a turn.
      • Runed Blade: your company fights a third larger.
      • War Banner: shrug off the first loss in each battle.
      • Cartographer's Map: never lost in fog, storms spoil less, and it knows the smugglers' way round every border garrison.
      • Hearth Charm: fever passes you by.

      The Gloom

      The Spire pulses every fourth round and the Gloom track rises. At 4 wraiths walk, at 7 prices rise, at 8 the barrow passages are haunted, at 10 Gloom Hounds hunt, at 14 prices soar. When the peg reaches the last hole, the Spire wakes and the chapter is lost.

      Warden's eye

      Press V or the Warden's eye button to leave the board and see the Reach through your own eyes. The Look button switches between the modern view (painted characters, real-time shadows, dusk sky) and a retro view drawn in sixteen colours the way a 1985 EGA screen would have. Overland, ← and → (or A and D) turn you toward each road and ↑ (or W) rides it. Inside a barrow or the Spire you walk a grid: W and S step, A and D turn, Q and E sidestep, and Esc climbs back out. Step into the antechamber to fight its wights, through the tomb gate to face the Barrow King, and up the Spire's stairs to the crown. The map in the corner fills in as your torch shows the way.

      The ledgers

      Each Warden's scorecard keeps company, coin and provisions with a tens peg and a units peg, plus holes for relics, the ward and Spire progress, and sockets for shards. Press Ledger to have the Spire read yours aloud, Repeat to see the last message again, and click the picture window to hurry it along.
