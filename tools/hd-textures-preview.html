Obsidian Spire · HD textures

Procedural textures for the modern first-person mode, from js/fp/hd/textures-hd.js. Each card shows the colour map tiled 2×2 (scaled to fit, so seams would show at the middle cross), the normal map, the emissive map where there is one, and the texture on a sphere and a plane lit by a moving torch and a low dusk sun. Add ?only=name to see one texture at 1:1, or ?no3d to skip WebGL.
